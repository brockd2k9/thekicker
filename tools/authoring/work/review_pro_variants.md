# Variants for the most repeated scenes (17)

Offseason Training and Summer Break now end at college; the pros get six of each (cooldown 40 weeks, weights 0.75 and 0.5). Holder Chemistry gets five variants (cooldown 30, weight 0.5 each, the original too). The window line for these reads 'repeats'.


## Variants

### pro_offseason_focus_a The Empty Facility
- **Category:** training  
- **Window:** repeats (15-week cooldown)  
- **Cast:** (no cast)  
- **Premise:** (hand-written pro scene: no design row)

The season is over and the facility is nearly empty: a few rehab players, the strength staff and a coffee machine nobody cleans. You have a few weeks to build something before camp.

1. **Build the leg**: Squats, sled pushes and long kicks into the wind, five mornings a week. Your leg aches by lunch, and by June the ball sounds different coming off your foot.  
   *(power +5; ambition +2; intelligence +1; 2 SP; modifier fresh_legs)*
2. **Groove the stroke**: Five hundred kicks a week from the same three spots, every one charted on a clipboard. It's boring, and the stroke that comes out of it is boringly perfect.  
   *(accuracy +5; ambition +1; charisma +1; 2 SP; modifier dialed_in)*
3. **Train the head**: Breathing work, visualization and pressure games with the team psychologist, who makes you kick with a stadium's noise in your headphones. Your heart rate stops jumping by May.  
   *(resilience +5; intelligence +2; 2 SP; modifier focused)*
4. **Rebuild the body**: Core, hips and the small muscles nobody films, with the strength staff three days a week. You come out of it moving like a younger player and sleeping like a stone.  
   *(health +5; toughness +5; leadership +1; 2 SP; modifier confident)*

- **Premise:** (hand-written pro scene: no design row)

   *(power +5; toughness +1; ambition +1; 2 SP; modifier fresh_legs)*

   *(accuracy +5; ambition +2; 2 SP; modifier dialed_in)*
3. **Take the mental track**: The blue track, the strange one: meditation, sleep coaching and kicking with a buzzer that goes off at random. It's uncomfortable, and by camp nothing rattles you at all.  
   *(resilience +5; charisma +1; intelligence +1; 2 SP; modifier focused)*
4. **Take the conditioning track**: The red track: running, mobility and hours in the pool with the linemen. You're the fittest specialist in the building by camp, and the linemen stop calling you 'the kicker'.  
   *(health +5; social +1; toughness +4; 2 SP; Team +2; modifier confident)*
